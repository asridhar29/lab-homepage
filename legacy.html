Legacy & Impact

LeBron cares about more than just basketball. In 2018 he opened the I PROMISE School in Akron, Ohio for kids who need extra help.

The school is free and gives kids meals and other support too.

He also started something called More Than a Vote in 2020 to help get people registered to vote.
